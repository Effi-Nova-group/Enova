Every abandoned cart is a customer who wanted something you sell, got most of the way to buying it, and then left. Industry research consistently puts average cart abandonment at around 70%. For most online stores, that makes the checkout the single biggest source of recoverable revenue, which is often far cheaper to fix than buying more traffic.

Enova builds fully optimised online stores designed to maximise checkouts, minimise cart abandonment, and scale sales. Here's what actually moves the needle.

## Why Shoppers Abandon Their Carts

### Unexpected Costs

Shipping, taxes, and fees that only appear at the final step are the most commonly cited reason for abandonment. Shoppers feel misled, and they leave.

### Forced Account Creation

Requiring a password and account before purchase adds friction at exactly the wrong moment. Many shoppers will simply go somewhere that lets them check out as a guest.

### A Long or Confusing Checkout

Every additional form field, page load, and decision point is another opportunity to lose the sale, especially on mobile, where most e-commerce traffic now arrives.

### Trust and Security Doubts

If a store looks dated, loads slowly, or doesn't clearly show secure payment options, shoppers hesitate to hand over card details.

## Fixing the Checkout Itself

The highest-impact improvements usually happen inside the checkout flow:

- **Show total costs early.** Display shipping estimates on product and cart pages, or set a clear free-shipping threshold.
- **Offer guest checkout.** Let customers create an account after purchase, when it feels like a benefit rather than a barrier.
- **Cut form fields ruthlessly.** Use address autocomplete, remember details, and remove anything you don't genuinely need.
- **Add express payment options.** Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal let mobile shoppers finish in a couple of taps.
- **Display trust signals.** Secure checkout badges, clear return policies, and visible reviews reduce last-second doubt.

## Speed Is a Conversion Feature

A slow store loses sales before the shopper ever reaches the cart. Each extra second of load time measurably reduces conversion rates, and the effect is strongest on mobile connections. Image compression, lean code, and fast hosting aren't technical niceties. They are revenue levers.

## Win Back the Shoppers Who Still Leave

Even a perfect checkout won't convert everyone. A recovery system catches a meaningful share of the rest:

1. **Abandoned cart emails** sent within the first hour, then followed up over the next day or two.
2. **Retargeting ads** that show shoppers the exact products they left behind.
3. **Smart incentives** such as free shipping or a small discount, reserved for shoppers who need a nudge rather than offered to everyone.

AI-driven tools can predict which abandoners are most likely to return and tailor the timing and offer for each one, so you protect margins instead of discounting by default.

## Measure, Test, Repeat

Cart abandonment isn't fixed with a single redesign. Track your funnel step by step, find where shoppers drop off, and run controlled tests on each change. Small improvements at the checkout compound quickly, because every percentage point recovered applies to all of the traffic you already pay for.

## Turn More Visitors Into Customers

Your store doesn't necessarily need more visitors. It needs to convert more of the ones it already has. A checkout built for speed, trust, and simplicity is one of the fastest routes to growth an online business can take.
